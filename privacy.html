Jelly Jars Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Jelly Jars ("the game") is published by Kedai Game MY ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and the game has no user accounts. Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

Information collected by third-party services

The game uses the following services, which may automatically collect information from your device:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows ads in the game. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (analytics): helps us understand how the game is played, for example which levels are completed or failed. It collects app usage events, device information, the app instance ID and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK (advertising measurement): tells us whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to installs, using the advertising ID, app events (such as levels completed) and device information.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

How information is used

  • To show ads and measure their performance
  • To understand and improve gameplay, difficulty and stability
  • To measure the effectiveness of our own advertising

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Players in regions where consent is required are asked for consent when the game starts, and can change it later under Pause > Privacy settings.
  • Notifications: The game only sends reminders (such as lives being full) if you allow them. You can turn them off anytime in your device settings.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information sent by the services above is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
